Flash XiaoVault in 30 Seconds

Install the complete offline survival Wikipedia & emergency library directly from your web browser. Zero software, drivers, or terminal setup required.

🟢
Browser Ready: Web Serial API detected. Compatible with Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Opera, and Brave on Windows, macOS, Linux, and ChromeOS.
⚠️
Unsupported Browser: Web Serial is not supported on this browser (or on iOS/Android mobile browsers). Please open this page on a computer using Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
Step 1

Connect USB-C

Plug your Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-C6 into your computer using a standard USB-C data cable.

Step 2

Click Flash

Click the button below and pick your Xiao COM port from the browser prompt to install.

Step 3

Insert SD & Power

Insert your MicroSD card and plug into any portable phone charger power bank!

Web Serial is not supported on this browser. Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a desktop computer. Web Serial is only allowed over HTTPS or localhost.

💡 Flashing takes ~25 seconds. The firmware, bootloader, and partition table will be installed and verified automatically.

🔌 Hardware Wiring (XIAO to MicroSD Breakout)

Wire the 6 pins between your Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-C6 and standard SPI MicroSD card module:

MicroSD Pin XIAO ESP32-C6 Pin Function Wire Color (Typical)
VCC 3V3 3.3V Power Red
GND GND Ground Black
SCK / CLK D8 (GPIO 19) SPI Clock Yellow
MISO D9 (GPIO 20) SPI Master In / Slave Out Green
MOSI D10 (GPIO 18) SPI Master Out / Slave In Blue
CS D2 (GPIO 2) SPI Chip Select White
Pro Tip: If your Xiao COM port doesn't appear in the browser list, hold down the tiny physical BOOT button on the Xiao while plugging the USB-C cable into your computer. This forces the ESP32-C6 into native ROM bootloader mode.
