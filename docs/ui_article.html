Emergency Water Purification

⚠️ Pathogen Warning: Never ingest untreated wilderness surface water. Giardia, Cryptosporidium, E. coli, and rotaviruses cause rapid dehydration and organ failure within 48–72 hours.

Method 1: Rolling Boil (Gold Standard)

  • Bring water to a continuous rolling boil for 1 full minute (3 full minutes at altitudes > 6,500 ft / 2,000 m).
  • Kills 100% of protozoan cysts, bacteria, and viral pathogens.
  • Aeration: Pour between two clean cups to restore oxygen and eliminate flat taste.

Method 2: Unscented Household Bleach (5–6% Sodium Hypochlorite)

  • Clear water: Add 2 drops per quart / liter (8 drops per gallon).
  • Cloudy / murky water: Add 4 drops per quart / liter (16 drops per gallon).
  • Stir thoroughly and wait 30 minutes before drinking.
  • Water should have a faint chlorine smell; if not, repeat dose once and wait 15 minutes.

Method 3: Improvised Charcoal Biofilter (Pre-Filtration)

Layer materials from bottom to top inside a cut inverted bottle:

  • 1. Clean cloth / cotton bandana at spout.
  • 2. Finely crushed campfire hardwood charcoal (adsorbs toxins & pesticides).
  • 3. Clean fine sand.
  • 4. Coarse sand / gravel (catches sediment).

Note: Charcoal filters clarify water and remove chemicals, but DO NOT kill viruses. Always boil after filtering!
